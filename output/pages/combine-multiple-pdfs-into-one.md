## Combine PDFs and Images with Document Scanner & PDF Toolkit

1. **Launch the App and Start Merging.** Launch the Document Scanner & PDF Toolkit on your iPhone or iPad. Tap the option to merge PDFs. The app will prompt you to select the files you want to combine.
2. **Select Your Documents and Images.** Choose the PDF documents and individual images you wish to merge from your device's files or photo library. You can select multiple items at once. The app will import them into a single view.
3. **Arrange Pages in Order.** Once your files are imported, you will see all the pages in a list. Drag and drop individual pages to reorder them exactly as you need them. This ensures your final document flows correctly.
4. **Rotate or Delete Pages.** If any page is scanned upside down or sideways, tap on it to access editing options and rotate it. You can also delete any unwanted pages from the merged document before saving.
5. **Save or Share Your Combined PDF.** After arranging and editing your pages, tap to confirm. The app will then create your single, multi-page PDF. You can then save it to your device, share it via AirDrop, Mail, or directly to cloud services like iCloud Drive, Google Drive, or Dropbox.

- Before you start, gather all the PDFs and images you intend to combine into a single folder on your device. This makes selection faster.
