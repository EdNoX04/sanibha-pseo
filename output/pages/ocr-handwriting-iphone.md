## Convert Handwritten Notes to Text on iPhone

1. **Scan Your Handwritten Notes.** Open Document Scanner & PDF Toolkit and choose the camera icon to start a new scan. Position your iPhone over your notes. The app's automatic edge detection will find the page borders and crop the page for you. For multiple pages, use multi-page batch scanning to capture them all in one session as a single PDF.
2. **Enhance Readability with Filters.** After scanning, apply an enhancement filter to improve text clarity. The Black & White or Colour Boost filters can increase contrast, which helps OCR. Use the Shadow Removal filter if your lighting was uneven or if your hand cast a shadow during scanning. Better contrast leads to better OCR results for handwriting.
3. **Run On-Device Text Recognition (OCR).** Once your scan looks clear, start the text recognition (OCR) process. Document Scanner & PDF Toolkit performs text recognition on your iPhone. Your documents are not uploaded to a server. The app will process the scan and highlight the text it recognizes. Recognition accuracy for handwriting depends heavily on how neat and high-contrast the writing is.
4. **Copy or Export the Recognized Text.** After OCR is complete, you can review the recognized text. Tap to select specific words or paragraphs, then copy the text to your clipboard. You can also export the entire recognized text as a .txt file. This file can then be opened in any text editor or word processor on your iPhone or computer.
5. **Share Your Digitized Notes.** Save your scanned document as a PDF, which will include the OCR layer. You can then share your PDF or the exported .txt file via AirDrop, Mail, or directly save it to cloud services like iCloud Drive, Google Drive, or Dropbox for easy access on other devices.

## Tips for Better Handwriting OCR Results

- Use dark ink on light-colored, unlined paper for maximum contrast. Avoid pencils or light-colored pens.
- Write neatly in block letters if possible. Cursive handwriting is much harder for OCR software to accurately recognize.
- Ensure good, even lighting when scanning. Avoid direct glare or shadows, as these can obscure parts of your text.
- Hold your iPhone steady and parallel to the page to minimize distortion and help the app detect text more clearly.
- Review the OCR results carefully. No OCR is perfect, especially for handwriting, so proofread and correct any errors.


## Frequently asked questions

### Can an iPhone accurately read and convert handwritten notes to text?

Yes, an iPhone can convert handwritten notes to text using OCR apps like Document Scanner & PDF Toolkit. Accuracy depends on the legibility of the handwriting, contrast, and lighting. Neat, block-printed text will yield the best results, while cursive is more challenging for OCR.

### How do enhancement filters improve contrast for light or uneven handwriting?

Enhancement filters, such as Black & White or Colour Boost, increase the visual difference between the ink and the paper. The Shadow Removal filter can eliminate dark areas caused by uneven lighting. This improved contrast helps the OCR engine distinguish letters more clearly, leading to better recognition accuracy for light or faded handwriting.

### Why does cursive handwriting produce lower OCR accuracy than neat block print?

Cursive handwriting is harder for OCR because letters are often joined, and individual letter forms vary greatly between writers. Block print letters are separate and have more consistent shapes, making them easier for the OCR software to identify and convert into digital text.

### How does built-in iOS Live Text perform on handwriting compared to dedicated app OCR?

iOS Live Text can recognize text in photos and the camera view, including clear handwriting. It works well for copying short sections. However, dedicated app OCR, like in Document Scanner & PDF Toolkit, often provides more control with enhancement filters and can export entire documents as editable .txt files, which Live Text cannot do directly.
