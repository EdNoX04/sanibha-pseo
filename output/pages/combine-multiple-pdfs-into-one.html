How to Combine Multiple PDFs into One Document

Quick answer: To combine multiple PDFs into one document on iOS, select your files in Document Scanner & PDF Toolkit or the built-in Files app. Use Document Scanner & PDF Toolkit if you need to drag pages into a custom order, rotate upside-down sheets, or delete unwanted pages before exporting the merged file.

When you need to send a single document, receiving parts scattered across separate files causes delays. A typical scenario involves three separate contract addendums in PDF format and two photo attachments sitting in your Files app that must be consolidated into one structured presentation before signing or emailing.

This guide covers how to combine multiple PDFs and standalone images into a single organised PDF on your iPhone or iPad. It details the step-by-step process for arranging, rotating, and pruning pages before saving, as well as native iOS options and critical privacy considerations.

How to Merge PDFs and Images Using Document Scanner & PDF Toolkit

  1. Import your PDFs and images. Open Document Scanner & PDF Toolkit and select the merge feature. Choose the PDF files and image attachments you want to bring together. You can select multiple items at once from your local device storage or cloud providers.
  2. Drag pages into your desired order. Review the thumbnail grid displaying all pages from the selected documents. Tap and drag individual pages to position your cover sheet, contract addendums, and image exhibits in the exact sequence you require.
  3. Rotate or delete individual pages. Inspect your pages for orientation errors or accidental duplicates. Tap to rotate sideways or upside-down sheets so every page reads upright, and remove blank or unneeded pages before finalising the file.
  4. Export and share the combined PDF. Save your combined document as a single PDF. You can send it directly through Mail or AirDrop, save it back to the Files app, or export it to iCloud Drive, Google Drive, or Dropbox. If you need to shrink the resulting document, you can compress PDF file size separately.

App Features Built for Merging Documents

  • Merge PDFs: Lets you combine several PDFs and images into one cohesive file, change the page order by dragging thumbnails, and delete or rotate individual pages before saving.
  • Multi-page batch scanning: Allows you to scan fresh physical pages alongside existing digital files, grouping every sheet directly into your multi-page project without leaving the session.

Tips for Clean PDF Document Assembly

  • Check image orientations prior to exporting so that receipts and photo exhibits match the portrait layout of standard contract pages.
  • Prune blank separator sheets or repeated cover pages before generating the final file to avoid sending bloated attachments.
  • If you need to extract editable paragraphs from your combined records later, you can convert scanned images to text or convert PDFs to Word using dedicated tools.

Frequently asked questions

How do I merge multiple PDF files on my iPhone or iPad?

You can merge files using Document Scanner & PDF Toolkit by selecting your PDFs, arranging the pages in thumbnail view, and exporting the result. Alternatively, you can select the files inside the native iOS Files app, tap More (•••), and select Create PDF.

Can I mix image files and PDFs into the same combined document?

Yes. Document Scanner & PDF Toolkit allows you to select both PDF files and image formats together. They will appear in a single unified page grid where you can position photos alongside text pages.

How do I rearrange the page order or delete unwanted pages before saving?

Inside Document Scanner & PDF Toolkit, open the merge tool to see all imported pages as thumbnails. You can hold and drag any page into a new position, rotate skewed sheets, or tap the delete option on individual pages prior to saving.

Can I combine PDFs using built-in iOS tools without downloading an app?

Yes. In the iOS Files app, tap the More button, choose Select, pick the files you want to merge, tap More (•••) at the bottom, and select Create PDF. However, you cannot reorder or rotate pages during this process.

Does combining PDFs upload my files to an online server?

No, not when using Document Scanner & PDF Toolkit or the iOS Files app. Merging runs locally on your iPhone or iPad, so your documents remain private unless you choose to upload them to a third-party cloud provider.
